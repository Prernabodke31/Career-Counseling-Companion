PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
Students and fresh graduates face serious difficulty in choosing the right career
path. Traditional career counseling is:

  - Generic and not tailored to individual academic performance, GPA, or grades
  - Disconnected from real-time labor market trends (salaries, in-demand skills)
  - Unable to assess personality, aptitude, and interests as a holistic profile
  - Expensive or inaccessible for students in developing regions

Without personalized, data-driven guidance, students make uninformed decisions
that lead to career dissatisfaction, skills mismatches, and wasted education.

================================================================================
PROPOSED SOLUTION
================================================================================

An Agentic AI-powered Career Counseling Companion using IBM Granite
(ibm/granite-3-8b-instruct) on IBM WatsonX.ai that delivers:

1. PERSONALIZED CAREER RECOMMENDATIONS
   - Analyzes student GPA, subject grades, skills, interests, and 3 psychometric
     assessment scores to recommend Top 3 Career Pathways with match reasoning.

2. TRIPLE ASSESSMENT ENGINE
   - Interest Assessment   : 10 questions across 10 dimensions
   - Aptitude Assessment   : 8 questions across 8 dimensions
   - Personality Assessment: 8 questions across 8 dimensions
   - Scored 1-5 Likert scale, normalized to 0-100 per dimension
   - Injected into IBM Granite as structured JSON

3. AI CHAT COUNSELOR
   - /api/career/chat endpoint powered by IBM Granite
   - Maintains last 6 conversation exchanges for context-aware multi-turn guidance

4. LABOR MARKET INTELLIGENCE
   - JSearch API (RapidAPI) with curated fallback data
   - 10 hot roles, salary ranges $75k-$190k USD
   - Sector growth rates: AI/ML 40%, Cybersecurity 32%, Data Science 28% by 2030

5. SKILLS GAP ANALYZER
   - /api/career/skills-gap endpoint using IBM Granite
   - Identifies missing skills, recommends certifications, estimates time-to-close

6. FULL-STACK WEB APPLICATION
   - React 18 + Vite 5 frontend (8 pages)
   - FastAPI 0.115 + Python 3.12 backend
   - SQLite database (zero-setup, auto-created)
   - JWT authentication

================================================================================
